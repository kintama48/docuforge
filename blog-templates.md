### Content Structure for Each Post

Every blog post should follow this:

* **H1:** Keyword-rich title
* **Intro (100 words):** State the problem, hint at the solution
* **Body:** Practical, code snippets where relevant, comparisons with real benchmarks
* **CTA:** "Try DocuForge free" or link to playground/docs
* **Internal links:** Cross-link between posts
